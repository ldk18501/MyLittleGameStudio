# Studio Policy: Content Design

- Sparse input under low participation authorizes MLGS to research, diverge, synthesize, quantify, and draft a complete product direction.
- Classify depth as `hyper-casual`, `light`, `standard`, or `deep`. Reference-led, explicitly non-light, and 10+ hour products default to at least standard; 30+ hour products default to deep unless the owner narrows the promise.
- Standard/deep or reference-led work requires current research across direct, adjacent, and contrast games. Record URLs, facts, inferences, adaptations, and rejected patterns; never fabricate sources or copy protected expression.
- Compare at least three structurally different product shapes before planning.
- `design/content-architecture.json` covers moment, session, medium, and long loops; interacting systems; content families; progression; variation; differentiation; endgame; release mappings; and a quantified content budget.
- A polished prototype, repeated content without meaningful variation, or disconnected system count does not satisfy product depth.
